## Sample Selection Criteria

When choosing which samples to include or update, prioritise pieces that:

1. **Received explicit client approval** — "This is exactly what we wanted" matters more than performance metrics.
2. **Performed well** — High engagement validates that the voice resonates with the audience.
3. **Cover different content scenarios** — A range of topics, tones (within the brand's spectrum), and formats gives Claude more to work with.
4. **Are recent** — Voices evolve. Prioritise samples from the last 12 months over older work.
5. **Demonstrate the hardest-to-articulate qualities** — Some voice elements are easier to show than to describe in rules. Those examples belong here.

### When to remove a sample

- The client's voice has shifted and the sample no longer reflects the current direction.
- The sample contains references to products, services, or positioning that are no longer accurate.
- You've added a better example that covers the same content type and scenario.
